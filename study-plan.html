📚 30-Day Payroll Compliance Study Plan

A structured beginner-friendly roadmap to build knowledge in Malaysian Payroll, EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB taxation, payroll audit, and payroll compliance practices.

🌱 Week 1: Payroll Fundamentals

  • Understand the payroll process end-to-end.
  • Learn key payroll terminology.
  • Study employee lifecycle and payroll records.
  • Review the Employment Act 1955 basics.
  • Create summary notes.

💰 Week 2: Statutory Contributions

  • Study EPF (KWSP) contribution rules.
  • Learn SOCSO contribution categories.
  • Understand EIS requirements.
  • Practice contribution calculations.
  • Create a payroll deduction template.

📈 Week 3: PCB & Payroll Processing

  • Understand PCB (Monthly Tax Deduction).
  • Learn payroll calculations.
  • Study gross pay vs net pay.
  • Review payroll reports.
  • Practice payroll scenarios.

🛡️ Week 4: Payroll Compliance & Audit

  • Understand payroll internal controls.
  • Learn payroll audit procedures.
  • Review common payroll errors.
  • Study compliance risks.
  • Build a personal payroll reference guide.
